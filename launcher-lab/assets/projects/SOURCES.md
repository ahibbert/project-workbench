# Project thumbnail sources

The launcher thumbnails are cropped or scaled from project-owned outputs rather
than recreated illustrations.

- `gamlss-longitudinal/r-output.webp`: `Rplots.pdf` from the
  `gamlss.longitudinal` working repository.
- `distribution-playground/live-plot*.webp`: the live BCPE density SVG produced
  by Distribution Playground.
- `copula-paper/paper-page*.webp`: page one of arXiv preprint `2410.11892`.
- `gamlss-longitudinal-paper/manuscript-output*.webp`: the generated ACTG
  treatment-effects table from the working manuscript outputs.
- `housing-note/chart*.webp`: `latest-state-prices-1.png` from the rendered post.
- `ndis-note/chart*.webp`: `district-outcomes-spend-scatter-1.png` from the
  rendered post.
- `bulk-billing-note/chart*.webp`: `national-trend-1.png` from the rendered post.
- `workbench-blog/chart-collage*.webp`: a composite of the three published-note
  charts above.
