# Project artwork and chart sources

The active site reuses project-owned artwork and charts from published posts.

- `in-another-life/icon.webp`: the current `AppIcon-1024.png` in the In Another Life app assets.
- `faintburn/icon.webp`: `assets/generated/app_icon_1024.png` in the Faintburn (space-2d) project.
- `housing-note/post-preview*-20261020.webp`: a browser capture of the title, introduction and publication details of the [live housing post](https://blog.aydins-workbench.com/posts/housing-prices-first-pass/). Its published charts still appear malformed, so the preview uses the post header instead.
- `ndis-note/chart*-20261020.webp`: [baseline outcome levels by participant cohort and theme](https://blog.aydins-workbench.com/posts/ndis-outcomes-spend-service-access/index_files/figure-html/baseline-theme-summary-1.png), replacing the older district outcomes/spending scatter plot.
- `bulk-billing-note/chart*-20261020.webp`: [national bulk-billing trend](https://blog.aydins-workbench.com/posts/bulk-billing-changes-impact/index_files/figure-html/national-trend-1.png).

The two chart sources and the post preview were fetched from the live blog on 20 October 2026 and
resized proportionally, keeping the full plots and labels or post header. Dated filenames ensure
existing visitors receive the refreshed previews.

Earlier imagery sources are documented in `launcher-lab/assets/projects/SOURCES.md`.
