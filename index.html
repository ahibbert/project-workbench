About me.

My research focuses on flexible statistical models for longitudinal and public-health data, with an emphasis on tools that people can actually use. I am especially drawn to public-interest questions about health, access and services; away from the formal work, I build apps, games and public data notes wherever a messy problem could use a clearer interface.
